# Destination content review

Reviewed: 2 September 2026

Questory's launch routes are approximate, bundled suggestions rather than
turn-by-turn navigation or promises of safety. The app tells runners to remain
in public pedestrian areas, obey current signs, use designated crossings, and
stop when traffic, weather, construction, crowds, or an event make a route
unsuitable. A runner may skip every quest.

## Nha Trang

The Coastal Morning Run stays on the central Trần Phú waterfront and returns
along the same approximate line. Vietnam Tourism describes Trần Phú Beach as
the city's central beach with a promenade, sculpture gardens, and ornamental
planting. Khánh Hòa reporting also documents a public 2026 walking event from
the Trầm Hương Tower area along Trần Phú and back. This supports the route's
use of the public waterfront as plausible destination content.

## Ho Chi Minh City

The River and City Lights route links Nguyễn Huệ Walking Street and Bạch Đằng
Park. Ho Chi Minh City Tourism identifies Nguyễn Huệ as a public walking and
recreation space ending at Bạch Đằng. Government reporting describes Bạch Đằng
as a public riverfront park used for exercise. A 2025 city report also explains
that pedestrian bridges were planned to reduce traffic conflict between the
walking street and riverfront. Because construction and access can change, the
pack does not assume a particular bridge is open: it explicitly requires the
runner to use the currently designated crossing or pedestrian bridge.

## Verification boundary

The text and approximate geometry have been desk-reviewed against the public
sources above, and automated tests check that declared distance agrees with the
bundled polyline. Neither route has received an on-foot field inspection by the
Questory team. Field verification and a final event/construction check remain
required before presenting either route as demo-ready.
